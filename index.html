How much can I get?

Up to 120% of your car's current market value, as valued by the lender. Your existing car loan is closed from this amount, and the rest is paid into your bank account.

Why do I need 12 EMIs paid?

Lenders want to see at least a year of on-time repayments before they take over your loan and add a top-up. It shows them you repay reliably.

Will I have to give up my car?

No. You keep using your car. The new lender is recorded on the RC as the financier until the loan is repaid, just like your current loan.

Does checking eligibility affect my credit score?

No. Answering the questions on this page does not run a credit check. The lender checks your credit only when your case is formally logged in.

How long does it take?

Once you've shared all your documents, many lenders give a decision within about 48 hours. Disbursal timing depends on the lender.
